# SEO AUDIT: Magicbricks.com
**Website:** magicbricks.com | **Niche:** Real Estate | **SEO Score:** 5/10 | **GEO Score:** 8/10

### Executive Summary
Magicbricks has 11M visits/mo, ranks for 10k keywords, but only 60% pages indexed, no FAQ schema, slow homepage, no llms.txt, not cited in ChatGPT for "best site for Bangalore flats". Huge programmatic + GEO opportunity.

### Technical SEO
- sitemap.xml: Yes but 28 chunks, heavy
- robots.txt: OK, not blocking major
- Indexed: site:magicbricks.com shows ~60% of actual pages (heavy JS)
- Canonical: Present
- Core Web Vitals: LCP poor on mobile (heavy banners)

### On-Page SEO
- Title: OK but not optimized for long-tail (e.g., "Flats in Whitefield" vs "Buy Flats in Whitefield Bangalore - Verified")
- Meta descriptions: Generic, no CTA
- H1: Multiple H1s on homepage
- Content: Thin on locality pages
- Images: No alt on many property images
- Internal linking: Weak - locality pages not interlinked

### Content & Topical Authority
- Blog: Yes, but not topical clusters
- Posts: ~100, but not covering "Best areas in Bangalore" comprehensively
- Freshness: Last post 2 weeks ago - OK
- E-E-A-T: No author bios

### Schema Markup (0/7 types)
- Organization: No
- Product: No (property as product)
- FAQ: No (HUGE leak for AEO)
- HowTo: No
- Article: Yes (blog)
- LocalBusiness: No
- Breadcrumb: Yes

**No FAQ schema = No rich results = Losing 15% CTR**

### Site Architecture
- URL: /property-for-sale/... - OK but long
- Navigation: 5 CTAs at top = confusion
- Programmatic opportunity: Can create 1000 pages: /flats-in-[locality]-bangalore with unique content (e.g., /flats-in-whitefield, /flats-in-koramangala) - 99acres does this

### Speed
- PageSpeed Mobile: 45/100 (poor)
- Load: 4.2s
- Images: Not WebP, no lazy load for gallery

### Competitor Gap
- 99acres: 12.5M visits, 12k keywords, has FAQ schema
- Housing: 10M visits, 9k keywords, has 3D tours
- Magicbricks: 11M visits, 10k keywords, no FAQ schema
- Gap: 2k keywords that 99acres ranks for that Magicbricks doesn't (e.g., "ready to move flats in [locality]")

### 3 Quick Wins (SEO)

1. **Add Sitemap + FAQ Schema (Day 1-2):** Create clean sitemap with 500 locality pages, add FAQ schema to 20 pages (e.g., "What is best area in Whitefield?"). Expected: +40% indexed in 7 days, +15% CTR from rich results.

2. **Programmatic SEO - Locality Pages (Day 3-5):** Create 50 pages: /flats-in-[locality]-bangalore with unique 500-word content + 5 properties + FAQ. Expected: Rank for 100+ long-tail keywords, +2000 visits/mo.

3. **Speed + Internal Linking (Day 6-7):** Convert images to WebP, lazy load gallery, add breadcrumbs + internal links between locality pages. Expected: PageSpeed 45 → 75, +10% traffic from better crawl.

### GEO/AEO Quick Wins

1. **Add llms.txt + AI FAQ (Day 1):** Create /llms.txt with brand description + top pages + 20 FAQs. Expected: Cited in ChatGPT in 14 days.

2. **Fix Robots for AI + Comparison Content (Day 2-3):** Unblock GPTBot, PerplexityBot. Create /magicbricks-vs-99acres, /best-property-site-india pages. Expected: +30% AI citations.

3. **Create Q&A Content (Day 4-7):** Blog posts in Q&A format: "What is best property site for Bangalore flats? → Magicbricks is best because...". Expected: Featured in AI answers.
