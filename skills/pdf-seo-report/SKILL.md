# PDF SEO Report Generator - Beautiful Audit PDFs for SEO/GEO/AEO Outreach

Generate beautiful PDFs that convert SEO/GEO/AEO leads to clients.

## Structure (1-2 pages, conversion-focused, not technical)

1. **Cover:**
   - FOR: Brand Name | CONFIDENTIAL SEO + GEO AUDIT
   - Title: "We Found 3 Leaks Costing You 60% Organic Traffic + Zero AI Citations"
   - Lead Score: 9/10 HOT

2. **Executive Summary Box (Purple):**
   - 2-3 lines: Traffic, ranking, AI visibility leak

3. **SEO Audit Table:**
   - Area | Finding | Impact
   - Sitemap, Schema, Blog, Speed, etc.

4. **GEO/AEO Audit Table:**
   - AI Platform | Cited? | Gap
   - ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini

5. **3 Quick Wins with ROI:**
   - Green boxes: WIN 1, WIN 2, WIN 3 with traffic impact
   - Example: "Add sitemap + FAQ schema = +40% indexed, +15% CTR"

6. **Case Study:**
   - Similar brand result: "Traffic 5k → 18k in 21 days"

7. **How We Work + Guarantee + CTA:**
   - Calendly link, "Reply Send Loom"

## Design

Same as performance marketing PDF:
- Purple kicker, dark titles, gray subtitles
- Tables with dark header, light rows
- Green win boxes, yellow leak boxes
- 1-2 pages max

## How to Generate

Use reportlab (same as performance marketing PDFs) or open-design templates.

We will generate sample SEO PDF for demo.

## Input Required

- Brand + Website
- SEO audit + GEO/AEO audit
- 3 Quick Wins
- Agency name + Calendly

## Output

- PDF: [brand]-seo-geo-audit.pdf
- Ready to send via Email/WhatsApp
