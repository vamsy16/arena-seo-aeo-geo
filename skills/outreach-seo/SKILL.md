# Outreach SEO - Turn SEO/GEO/AEO Audit into Replies

Convert SEO audit into outreach that gets replies, not "We are SEO agency".

## 3 Frameworks

### Template A: Roast Audit (Best for SEO)

"Saw you rank page 3 for 'handmade bags' but only 24% pages indexed (120/500), no sitemap, no FAQ schema. Competitor Zouk competitor Mokobara ranks page 1 with 100% indexed + FAQ rich results. Found 3 leaks costing you 60% organic traffic - want 60-sec Loom?"

### Template B: Value Loom Script (30 sec)

"Hey [Name], checked your site - you have 3 blog posts, last post 6 months ago, no FAQ schema. Quick wins: Add sitemap + 10 FAQs with schema + 5 locality pages. We did this for [Similar] - traffic 5k → 18k in 21 days. Want teardown?"

### Template C: FOMO + AI Angle (Best for GEO/AEO - 2026 Blue Ocean)

"Hi [Name], you rank page 2 on Google for 'best skin clinic Bangalore' but you're NOT cited in ChatGPT or Perplexity when people ask same question - losing 40% of searches that now happen in AI. Your competitor Oliva IS cited. We fix GEO/AEO - make you visible in ChatGPT in 7-14 days with llms.txt + FAQ schema. Want Loom showing your AI gap?"

This AI angle gets 3x more replies in 2026 because GEO is new and 95% agencies don't offer it.

## Rules

- Never start with "We are SEO agency"
- Mention specific finding: indexed %, schema count, blog count, AI cited yes/no
- Mention competitor
- Soft CTA: "Want Loom?" not "Book call"

## Output

3 templates + subject lines + personalization tokens.
